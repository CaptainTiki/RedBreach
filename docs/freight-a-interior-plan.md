## Walkthrough feedback behind this pass

The user found the A loop's approximately 1.5 km traversal promising and accepted its large room footprints, provided the rooms gain meaningful interiors. The walk felt too flat, and aligned corridor openings created too many straight through-views. Requested direction: divide some departments into smaller rooms, add substantial upstairs portions and offset entrances/exits with selected corridor bends.

The saved A test recorded 1,467.9 m and 336.9 seconds (5:37 elapsed), including an A8 visit and selector interactions, ending with freight clearance and no power. Elapsed time is not a pure walking-speed measurement. The current paper trace is about 1,314 m for the required A route, hub to hub, excluding A8, Maintenance, exploration and combat. Its waypoint convention differs from the original 1,038 m room-centre estimate and the actual player recording; it does not establish an exact increase or a new playtest time. The populated level's ten-minute target remains a later measurement.

## The built A walk

1. **A1 Staff Intake / 0 m.** Enter the east side near its north end into reception. Turn through the screening partition, then leave the staff area near the southwest corner. An optional north office gives the department a smaller room without adding another required task.
2. **A2 Inspection / 0 to -2 m.** Arrive on a longer southeast entry deck. Descend onto the lower work floor, pass through an offset inspection doorway, and leave from the northwest side. Keep the selector and local Tr1 cache in A2. North rooms form the circuit/control area and a separate inspection bay. The north corridor bends east toward the existing blocked-door/bypass junction.
3. **A3 Records / 0 to +4 m.** Door 1 remains sealed. Reach Records from the bypass/door-2 junction through an offset east entrance. Enter below the upper walkway, turn around the archive partition and climb two flights to the north upper walkway. Lower partitions meet the deck underside where they overlap it; they must not cut through the upper route.
4. **A7 Supervisor / +4 m.** Follow a dogleg from A3's upper exit into an elevated admin/waiting area, then turn through a second doorway into the supervisor's office. Collect R at the desk. A7 remains the card room, now upstairs; there is no additional playable ground-floor route beneath A7 in this pass. Return along the same corridor and stairs to door 2.
5. **A4 Dispatch / +2 m.** Door 2 and its approach climb stay in place; a bend then brings you through the southwest lobby entrance. The lobby leads past freight card K into Dispatch. Continue through the interior partition to the choice between the existing BcD ladder and the A5 exit vestibule. A wall separates that vestibule from the entry lobby, making the route past Dispatch clear. K remains a visible pickup before the split, not an additional automatic gate.
6. **A5 Watch Gallery / +2 to +6 m.** Arrive near the northeast corner and climb two flights. The +6 m floor contains a watch room/gallery rather than just a stair landing. Cross it, pass the internal doorway and leave from the southwest side. The original lower south exit is sealed; the return connection is upstairs.
7. **A6 Clearance / +6 to 0 m.** An upper dogleg arrives on a substantial northwest office mezzanine. Cross the deck and descend three flights, with level intermediate landings, into the lower office department. Leave near the southeast corner and follow the existing S1 return to the hub. This six-metre descent replaces old T5's two-metre entry descent.
8. **A8 Annex / -2 m, optional.** Keep its existing connection and height. Divide it into a vestibule and two equipment/storage rooms, with an offset doorway into the main room. No mandatory pickup or new reward is assigned here yet.

The normal height sequence is hub 0 -> A2 -2 -> Records 0 -> Supervisor +4 -> Records 0 -> Dispatch +2 -> Watch +6 -> Clearance 0 -> hub 0. Room-to-room passages on the upper route are enclosed; views down occur inside the rooms. Guards protect open deck edges and stair approaches. All required routes are reversible; no new jump, forced drop, timer, key or gate is introduced.
