# Onboarding / security induction — provisionally Level 1

Status: 2026-09-22. Stage 1 of the agreed design process (see
`level-structure-notes.md`): the walkthrough is agreed in conversation, before
any spatial paper plan exists. Next stage is the 2D top-down pass. No geometry
built yet — this is not the new lab itself, it's the record of what the lab
needs to contain.

This also resolves an open item from `project-brief.md`: **player role is
Mars colony security personnel.** That gives a plain reason to be armed and
posted at an armory. The colony's broader campaign objective (aliens, the
long-presence discovery, eventual evacuation) is unaffected; this only settles
who the player is day to day.

## Campaign role and feel

Provisionally **Level 1**, ahead of the freight-access mission (level 2/3).
Where freight assumes the player already knows movement and combat, this
level is where that competence gets established — first through the tuned
Movement Gym / Combat Gym / Pistol Gym values, now through actual authored
content wearing those systems.

It is also the direct test bed for [study 02](architecture-reference-study-02-rooms.md)
(room density/count) and [study 03](architecture-reference-study-03-rhythm.md)
(rate of change, verticality, lighting variation, active machinery,
crawlspaces). If the combined language doesn't survive being walked here,
that's recorded before any freight remodeling is attempted, per the
Doom 3-research plan agreed earlier this session.

## Mission goal

Complete induction (sign in, collect comms, draw a sidearm, get familiar with
it) then receive a first assignment from Dispatch — investigate a reported
missing person — and depart through an airlock/elevator to load the next
level.

## Approved route walkthrough

1. **Arrival.** A quiet foothold room, same convention as freight's Receiving
   — orient before any pressure. Exact arrival framing (why the player is
   here, what's immediately behind them) is open.
2. **Reception.** A staffed sign-in desk with chairs and terminals — built to
   read as lived-in whether or not an NPC happens to be present when the
   player passes through. Comms/radio pickup happens here (or in an adjoining
   nook off reception; undecided until the spatial pass).
3. **Transit to the armory.** Short connecting section(s); a candidate first
   place for a duck/crawlspace beat since crouch already exists in the
   controller.
4. **Armory.** The player's actual guard post — draw the sidearm here.
   Whether armor/suit pieces are also issued here is still open.
5. **Range, in back of the armory.** Static shootables, cover to duck
   behind, ammo pickups, and a first teaching moment for ducking to dodge
   incoming fire from a scripted ranged threat. Deliberately kept small and
   placeholder-scale for now — expand only if the area proves itself in play.
   Whether the preview enemy is the existing Spitter or a simpler stand-in is
   open.
6. **Transit through a couple more sections** toward Dispatch. A second
   corridor-rhythm sample, distinct from the reception→armory stretch.
7. **Dispatch.** A second reception-style desk; an NPC gives the first
   assignment — a reported missing person, location TBD in the spatial pass.
8. **Transit away from Dispatch** toward the airlock/elevator. Doesn't need
   to be new space — walking back through an earlier stretch (in reverse) is
   an explicit option, and doubles as a cheap FR-005-style return view if
   anything reads differently on the way back (lighting, a detail noticed
   the second time). Just needs enough distance that the exit doesn't feel
   like it's "in the next room" from Dispatch — roughly a minute's travel.
9. **Airlock or elevator.** Standard transition chamber per the existing
   airlock/elevator convention (occupied cabin, closed doors both sides,
   destination title on arrival). Ends this level; loads the missing-person
   mission next. Whether that next mission is freight itself or a new,
   separate level is undecided.

## Open questions carried into the spatial pass

- Reception/Dispatch NPCs: scripted presence, always-there, or intentionally
  sometimes-absent (desk still reads as staffed via its furnishing either
  way)?
- Range scope: confirm static-only vs. previewing a live enemy type now or
  later.
- Gear beyond the sidearm (armor/suit) — issued at the armory or not part of
  this pass?
- Missing-person destination: same level as freight, or a distinct new
  mission?
- Exact section counts/lengths for the two transit stretches — deferred to
  the 2D plan, informed by study 02's corridor-run reference numbers (median
  ~3.3 m, 90th percentile ~16 m between turns) as a loose tuning reference,
  not a target.

## Verticality and rhythm intent

Per study 03, expect small verticality and frequent change by default rather
than as an add-on: a riser at reception's desk, a step at Dispatch's
threshold, lighting that varies room to room, at least one active/audible
service element somewhere along the transit stretches. None of this is fixed
yet — it belongs to the spatial pass, where it can be drawn against the
actual room list above.
