## Working on a different machine

- The repo is `https://github.com/CaptainTiki/RedBreach.git`, branch `main`. Work is developed on a desktop and a laptop, so **never assume a path outside the repo**. Everything inside the repo is relative; everything outside it is per-machine.
- **Godot** is the only external dependency the tooling needs, and the version must match (4.7). Set it once per machine:
  `setx REDBREACH_GODOT "C:\path\to\godot.windows.opt.tools.64.exe"`
  Every `tools/rebuild-*.ps1` reads `$env:REDBREACH_GODOT`, falls back to the desktop's Steam path, and throws a message naming the path it tried. `-GodotPath` still overrides both. Do not hardcode a new machine's path into the scripts.
- **Python 3** runs the drawing and generator scripts in `tools/`. They need only the standard library plus whatever the existing drawing scripts already import; they write into `docs/` and never touch a map unless they are named `bootstrap-*` or `apply-*`.
- Both addons under `RedBreach/addons/` are committed and must be preserved. After a fresh clone, run `godot --headless --path RedBreach --import` once so textures and `.map` files get their `.import` sidecars, then run the relevant `tools/rebuild-*.ps1 -Validate`.
- **Check the work actually arrived**: a rebuild on a new machine should reproduce the recorded counts exactly. Architecture lab: `ARCH_LAB_BUILD: 151 brushes; save=0` then `ARCH_LAB_QA: 1037 checks; 0 failures`. A different number means the map or the section module did not travel, not that the check is wrong.
- Uncommitted work does not travel. Before switching machines, commit and push; after switching, `git pull` before touching anything.

## Version every commit
- The starting project version is `0.0.001`, stored in `RedBreach/project.godot` as `application/config/version`.
- The first commit containing this setup establishes `0.0.001`. Each subsequent commit increments the final numeric component by one: `0.0.002`, `0.0.003`, etc.

- Include the version change in the same commit as the work. Increment once per commit, not once per file or editing session.
- Before committing, verify the staged scope and version against the previous committed project version.

## Architecture lab

- The corridor language experiment lives in `docs/architecture-lab-plan.md` with two drawings: `docs/architecture-lab-section.png` (the section and its pieces) and `docs/architecture-lab-plan.png` (rhythm, kit and a provisional circuit). - Revision 06 is four reusable pieces, not one carved profile: FLOOR, WALL and CEILING make half a corridor and are mirrored; a RIB drops in every 4 m. Nine facets per half, five for the rib. No outward-rising facet the player can reach sits at 45 degrees: those are 51.34, 56.31 and 63.43. The remaining 45 degree chamfers are all overhangs and cannot be stood on. 45 is exactly the floor_max_angle default and gym_player.gd never assigns it, so it is a coin flip. Chamfers, not stepped setbacks.
- The rib runs floor to ceiling on a battered base: the toe sits exactly on the protected lane line at x = 2.00, so opposite toes define the 4.00 m clear width, the toe is vertical to knee height so the capsule never meets a slope low down, and the base then batters back 0.50 m onto the shaft at 51.34 degrees. The corridor is 6.00 m wide rather than 5.50 purely to buy that flare; the walking lane is unchanged at 4.00 m. An earlier draft stopped it at the plinth and was rejected - a pillar that ends halfway down a wall does not read as structure. Swap-outs replace the 1.25 - 2.50 m panel zone only, so the floor, ceiling and rib pieces are never touched.
- `tools/architecture_lab_section.py` is the single source of truth for the chains. Both drawing scripts import it and it self-checks for off-grid vertices, pieces that do not mate, a walkable slope, lane intrusion and a rib that does not project. Change the section there, never in a copy.
- Revision 06 is built and lit: one 24.5 m straight run, seven ribs at 4 m, plain W1 panels. 151 brushes, 1,037 validation checks pass. `RedBreach/maps/architecture_lab_01.map` is the editable source and `RedBreach/architecture/architecture_lab.tscn` the saved scene. Run `tools/rebuild-architecture-lab.ps1 -Validate -Capture`. `tools/bootstrap-architecture-lab.py` regenerates the map straight from the section chains and is never part of a normal rebuild.
- Two findings to preserve from that build. Registers separate by geometry and shadow, not tonal contrast - a charcoal plinth against pale infill turned the lower register into a black void. And facet count is nearly free while rib count is the cost, since each wall facet is one long extrusion down the whole straight.
- Materials split by ELEMENT, not by height register: rib is one texture zone toe to beam, the bay is another and means the whole bay - wall at every height plus the ceiling between beams - and the floor stays continuous Dark/texture_06. Splitting by register instead made the wall read as three unrelated bands. The structure is the LIGHTER zone (GreyPale 151) and the bay falls away behind it (GreyMedium 97); that matches the references and shrinks the jump to the dark floor from 98 brightness points to 44.
- Lighting is built. The rib beams at 3.50 and the ceiling soffit at 3.75 already form a 0.25 m coffer in every bay, so recessed strips cost six brushes and no cutting. Two strips per bay near the coffer EDGES, not down the middle, so the wash rakes the wall and the chamfers read. EVERY bay carries a fitting; a per-bay tier in S.BAY_LIGHTS (lit / off) decides whether it is on, so turning a bay into a broken fitting is a one-word change with no geometry edit. An OFF fitting must NOT use an emissive material - a strip that glows while its bay stays dark reads as a mistake, because a fixture that is emitting should be lighting something. The scene's light nodes are generated from the same dictionary. Emissive material at `textures/greybox/Emissive/texture_01.tres`, picked up through `mapping/architecture_lab_map_settings.tres`, which is a lab-only settings resource so the freight maps are untouched.
- Brightness lives in the lights, not the emissive brush. The corridor was bright because ambient sat at 0.75 with a directional sun in an interior; it is now ambient 0.13, a 0.18 fill with no shadows, SSAO on (radius 0.6, intensity 2.4), and OmniLight3D nodes under `Lighting`, a sibling of Geometry so rebuilds never touch them. Ambient is the strongest lever by far - 0.16 flattened the whole corridor straight back to the greybox look. Contrast between lit and off bays is managed with light RANGE (13 m against a 4 m bay pitch, so each fitting spills a bay and a half either side) rather than by raising ambient.
- The light node is NOT the fitting: the fitting face is at 3.625 but the node sits at 3.375, below the rib beam soffit. Sitting it just under the ceiling left the ceiling in shadow because light hits a large flat plane at a grazing angle from 0.17 m below and N.L collapses; dropping it lifted the measured ceiling band from 58.5 to 78.0 and spills further down the corridor as a bonus. Emissive materials do not illuminate anything in Godot without GI, so the strips are cosmetic and every photon comes from the OmniLight3D nodes.
- `tools/write-architecture-lab-scene.py` regenerates the lab scene from the section module. Run it after changing a bay tier or a lighting constant, then rebuild.
- Anything generated from a tier name must be generated from the table that defines it. A scene-writing helper keyed off hardcoded tier names silently wrote ZERO lights when the tiers were renamed, and two rounds of 'too dark' were a corridor lit by ambient alone. It now drives off S.LIGHT_TIERS and asserts it wrote at least one light.
- New textures need `godot --headless --path RedBreach --import` before a build, or func_godot cannot load the material.
- A player who drifts into a bay is stopped by the next rib, because the shaft stands 0.5 m proud. That is correct, not a bug - the bays are shelter, not a running lane. The validator asserts only that jamming into the wall never lifts the player.
- Corners, junctions and the circuit routing are still deliberately undecided; the circuit on `architecture-lab-plan.png` is labelled provisional and still carries revision 05 thinking. **The corner is the next piece and the part I am least sure of** - whether the chamfers mitre cleanly is untested. The plinth and panel registers below 2.50 m are what should carry into rooms; the upper wall and ceiling are corridor-specific.
